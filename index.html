Send finished short videos to your TikTok drafts

Ai_Video is a small personal tool. I make vertical videos (1080×1920) on my computer. When a video is ready, the Ai_Video uploader sends the MP4 file to my TikTok inbox as a draft. I then open TikTok, write the caption, choose a sound and publish it myself.

How it works

  1. Connect TikTok. Sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and allow Ai_Video to upload videos to your drafts.
  2. Upload. The Ai_Video uploader on your computer sends the MP4 file with the Content Posting API (video.upload, file upload).
  3. Review and post in TikTok. TikTok notifies you in the app inbox. Nothing is published without your action.

Connect TikTok

Start the Ai_Video uploader on your computer first — it opens this page with your sign-in link.

What Ai_Video accesses

  • user.info.basic — your display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload — to place a video into your TikTok drafts / inbox.

Ai_Video does not read your other videos, messages, followers or analytics, and does not publish anything on your behalf.
