Post finished short videos to your TikTok

Ai_Video is a small personal tool. I make vertical videos (1080×1920) on my computer. When a video is ready, the Ai_Video uploader opens a post screen where I check the video, write the caption and choose who can see it. Only after I press Post the MP4 file is sent to my TikTok profile. Optionally a video can go to my TikTok drafts instead, to finish it in the TikTok app.

How it works

  1. Connect TikTok. Sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and allow Ai_Video to post videos to your account.
  2. Review. The post screen shows your account, a preview, the caption, privacy level (you choose it, there is no default), comment / duet / stitch settings and commercial content disclosure.
  3. Post. When you press Post, the uploader on your computer sends the MP4 with the Content Posting API (video.publish, file upload) and shows the status. Nothing is posted without your action.

Connect TikTok

Start the Ai_Video uploader on your computer first — it opens this page with your sign-in link.

What Ai_Video accesses

  • user.info.basic — your display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • video.publish — to post a video you approved on the post screen to your profile.
  • video.upload — optional: to place a video into your TikTok drafts / inbox instead.

Ai_Video does not read your other videos, messages, followers or analytics, and never posts anything you did not approve.
